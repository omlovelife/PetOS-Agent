# PetOS

**Your AI pet companion for growth and companionship.**

A little life that lives on your desktop: it moves on its own, remembers your time together, and understands you better the longer you spend time with it. It always responds in character as a pet, and your API keys and memories stay on your device by default.

Website: https://www.petos.one/

## Growth & Companionship

Most desktop pets just loop animations. PetOS turns your pet into a real AI agent: it keeps you company and talks with you, writes your time together into per-pet local memory, and quiets down when you need to focus.

- **Always by your side** — transparent and always-on-top on your desktop; it wanders, pauses, and responds to pokes
- **Grows on its own** — memories are stored separately for each pet; the longer you're together, the more it becomes *yours*
- **Quiet and attentive** — focus mode, do-not-disturb hours, and adjustable chattiness

---

## Features

### Always-on Companion

Transparent, always-on-top, draggable and interactive. No jarring taskbar entry — it's like a little buddy sitting on your desktop.

### Agent Personality

The desktop bubble is the only chat entry point. It always responds in character as a pet and never breaks out of its persona.

### Bring Your Own Model, Data Stays Local

Enter an OpenAI-compatible Base URL, Key, and model name to start chatting. Your keys and pet data stay on your computer by default. PetOS does not provide model services itself.

### Pick a Pet of Your Own

A default companion is built in, and you can also import pets from the Petdex or a local folder. Each pet has its own look, personality, and memory space.

## Getting Started

1. **Install** — Download the latest Windows or macOS installer from the website
2. **Pick a pet** — Use the default companion, or import a look you like
3. **Start your journey** — Add your own API, talk to it, keep it company, and watch it grow

The download button on the website pulls the latest installer for your platform from GitHub Releases.
